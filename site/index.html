Where it's from

Four places, four crafts

26.2° N · 73.0° E

Jodhpur

Old-city dealers stack household brass and copper from Marwar villages by the kilo. Most of it is ordinary. We keep about one piece in twenty.

26.8° N · 75.6° E

Bagru

Chhipa printing families hand-carve teak blocks. Old blocks wear smooth after thousands of impressions, and new ones still come from the same benches.

23.7° N · 87.7° E

Birbhum

Women layer worn saris and stitch them into kantha with running stitch. Each quilt is different, and each one was used by a family.

19.1° N · 81.9° E

Bastar

Dhokra casters still use the 4,000-year-old lost-wax method: beeswax thread, clay mould, open fire. Made new, to order.

Shipping & duties

Before you buy

Will I pay anything on delivery?

No. We ship DDP (delivered duty paid) to the US, UK, EU, Canada and Australia. Import duty, the EU's per-item parcel duty and VAT where it applies are in the price you see.

How long does shipping take?

Each piece ships from Jaipur within 3 working days, by tracked express courier. Delivery is usually 6–10 working days after that.

Are these real antiques?

No, on purpose. Indian law bars private export of objects over 100 years old. We sell vintage pieces, roughly 20–80 years old, and new artisan work. Every listing gives an estimated decade.

Can I cook or drink from the brass?

Treat vintage brass and copper as decorative. Older pieces can contain lead, and we don't test for food safety.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send photos within 7 days and we refund in full. Every piece is double-boxed and wrapped in old newsprint and jute.

Can I return a piece I don't like?

Vintage lots are final sale because return freight to India costs more than most pieces. That's why every listing shows the wear and a condition grade.

First look at new lots

One email when a new edit is ready, about once a month. Pieces usually sell within a week.
