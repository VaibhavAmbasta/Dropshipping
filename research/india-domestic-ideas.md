# India-domestic businesses: urgent problems people already pay for

Research date: 1 October 2026. I picked ideas where the problem has a deadline or penalty attached, buyers already spend money on it, it recurs, and one person can start it without much capital.

## Ranked

| # | Business | The problem in numbers | Who pays, and how | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tax-notice desk for small businesses**: catch GST and income-tax mismatches before notices arrive, and draft replies when they do | AI-driven GST scrutiny is producing more automated notices (ASMT-10, DRC-01). The new Income Tax Act 2025 (from 1 Apr 2026) changed every TDS section code, and old codes now fail validation. Unanswered notices can carry penalties up to 100% of the tax | Business owner. ₹1–3k a month to monitor, or ₹3–15k per notice handled | You need a CA or enrolled GST practitioner to sign. Competes with local CAs, ClearTax and IndiaFilings |
| 2 | **Health-insurance claim recovery and pre-claim file check** | ₹30,000 cr of health claims rejected in FY25 (8% of claims). 73,729 complaints on Bima Bharosa up to Feb FY26. 32% of reimbursement rejections in Q4 2025 traced to illegible discharge summaries | Policyholder. ₹999 for a pre-submission file check, or a 10–20% success fee on recovered amounts | Check the rules on paid intermediaries before the Insurance Ombudsman. Large claims attract fraud attempts |
| 3 | **Safety and care subscription for elderly parents whose children live elsewhere** | 1.03 lakh seniors lost ₹4,005 cr to cyber fraud in 2025. Total reported fraud was ₹22,495 cr. Digital-arrest scams target the elderly. In-home care is about 45% of the elder-care market | The adult child, in a metro or abroad. ₹999–2,999 a month | Ops-heavy and built on trust. Start in one city |
| 4 | **Kit for complying with India's new data-protection law (DPDP)**, for clinics, schools, coaching centres, staffing firms and D2C brands | Consent Manager framework from 13 Nov 2026. Full compliance from 13 May 2027. No exemption for small firms. Penalties up to ₹250 cr | The business. ₹25–75k setup plus ₹2–5k a month | Small businesses ignore compliance until someone is actually fined. Sell to mid-size, data-heavy firms first |
| 5 | **Air-purifier rental in Delhi NCR**, seasonal | Pollution season starts now (Oct–Feb) | Households. ₹999–1,499 a month including filters | Seasonal churn after February, and each unit costs money up front |

## Avoid
Generic consumer apps, edtech, quick commerce, packaged food brands, and lending or fintech. They're either capital-heavy, already crowded, or regulated.
